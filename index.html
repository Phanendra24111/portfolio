Projects

GymVision AI

Computer vision, web app
84.8% validation accuracy across 19 exercise classes

A tool that watches a workout video and tells you how good your form is. MediaPipe extracts body pose from each frame, and an ST-GCN model trained on those skeleton sequences classifies the exercise across 19 classes, reaching 84.8% validation accuracy.

The model runs inside a Flask app that gives a form score, keeps a history of past sessions, and includes a chatbot powered by Groq's LLaMA for exercise questions. I also produced the research presentation, poster and architecture diagrams for it at VIT.

Built with Python, PyTorch, MediaPipe, ST-GCN, Flask, Groq API

WebDigestAI

October 2025, LLM application
Up to 70% less reading time, about 90% accurate, tested on 1000+ URLs

An article summarizer that reads both static and dynamic web pages and turns them into short answers you can ask follow-up questions about. It cut reading time by up to 70%.

Content is scraped with BeautifulSoup4 and WebBaseLoader (tested on 1000+ URLs), then served through a retrieval-augmented pipeline in LangChain with the Gemini API. Responses were about 90% accurate, and retrieval time dropped by around 40%.

Built with Python, LangChain, Gemini API, BeautifulSoup4, RAG
